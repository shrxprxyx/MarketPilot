# Coffee Subscription Plans

The Weekly plan sends one 340 gram bag every week for 16 dollars a bag. The Fortnightly plan sends a bag every two weeks for 17 dollars a bag. The Monthly Discovery plan sends three different single origin coffees, 150 grams each, for 29 dollars.

Subscribers can pause, skip a delivery or cancel at any time from their account page, with no fees. Changes must be made 48 hours before the next roast date.

Subscribers save 10 percent compared with one-off purchases and receive early access to limited seasonal roasts.
