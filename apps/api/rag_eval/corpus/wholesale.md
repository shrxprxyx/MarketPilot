# Wholesale Partner Program

Cafes, offices and restaurants can buy Brewline beans in bulk through the wholesale program. The minimum order is 10 kilograms, and partners receive tiered discounts: 15 percent from 10 kilograms, 22 percent from 50 kilograms a month.

Each wholesale partner gets a dedicated account manager, free barista training for up to four staff members, and branded menu cards and counter signs.

To apply, send your business name, expected monthly volume and delivery address to the wholesale team. Approval usually takes two business days.
