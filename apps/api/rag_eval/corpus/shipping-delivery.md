# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Orders placed before 2 pm on a weekday are roasted and dispatched the same day. Standard delivery takes three to five working days within the United States and costs 6 dollars, and it is free on orders over 40 dollars.

Express delivery arrives in one to two working days for 14 dollars. We currently ship to the US and Canada only. International customers can join the waiting list on our website.

Every parcel includes a tracking link by email. If a parcel is lost or arrives damaged, contact support within 14 days and we will send a replacement bag at no charge.
