# Social Media Guidelines

We post on Instagram four times a week and on TikTok twice a week. The best performing content is short behind-the-scenes roasting videos, farm stories and brew guides that last under 30 seconds.

Always reply to comments within 24 hours. Use the hashtags #BrewlineMorning and #SmallFarmBigFlavour. Reshare customer photos only after asking for permission in a direct message.

Never post about politics. Promotions run on the first Friday of each month and are announced the day before in Stories.
