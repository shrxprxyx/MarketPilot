# Sourcing and Sustainability

Brewline buys directly from 14 smallholder farms in Colombia, Ethiopia and Guatemala, paying at least 30 percent above the Fairtrade minimum price. Every bag lists the farm, altitude and harvest year.

Our roastery runs on 100 percent renewable electricity. Packaging is compostable and the shipping boxes use recycled cardboard. We aim to be carbon neutral across the full supply chain by 2027.

We publish an annual impact report that shows what we paid each farm and how many farmers benefited from our community projects such as water filtration and training.
