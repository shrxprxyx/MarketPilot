# Brewline Brand Voice

Brewline speaks like a knowledgeable friend behind the counter: warm, curious and never snobby. We explain coffee in plain words and avoid jargon unless we immediately explain it.

Preferred tone: friendly, upbeat, lightly humorous. Use short sentences and active verbs. Address the reader as "you". Emojis are welcome in social posts, but no more than two per post.

Words we avoid: "premium", "artisanal", "world-class" and any claim that a competitor's coffee is bad. We never shame customers for how they take their coffee, including with sugar or oat milk.
