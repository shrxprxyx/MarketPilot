# Returns and Refunds

If you are not happy with a bag of coffee, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we will refund the full price or send a different coffee. You do not need to return the opened bag.

Brewing equipment such as grinders and pour-over kettles can be returned unused within 60 days in the original box. We cover the return postage for faulty items.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and normally appear within five to seven business days.
