# Allergen and Product FAQ

Our beans are naturally free from gluten, nuts and dairy. The roastery does handle flavoured syrups that contain tree nuts, but these are kept in a separate room with dedicated equipment.

Decaf coffees are processed with the Swiss Water method, which uses only water and no chemical solvents. Decaf contains about 0.1 percent of the original caffeine.

Beans stay freshest for four weeks after the roast date printed on the bag. Store them in a cool, dark place in an airtight container and do not refrigerate them.
